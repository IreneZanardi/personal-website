Teaching

Course
User Experience Design
Master’s students

Academic Years
2022/23, 2023/24, 2024/25, 2025/26

Role
Teaching Assistant

Studying UX design means recognizing that tech is all about people. And as Frank Chimero stated, ‘People ignore design that ignores people.’

The success of any digital product depends on its usability and user experience. This course provides a hands-on, practical approach to creating accessible, inclusive and user-friendly digital artifacts. Students discover the significant impact of well-designed interfaces on performance and user satisfaction.

In addition, they learn how to identify user demands, build compelling interfaces, and balance various design options. They study UX best practices and usability testing through hands-on projects, seminars, and real-world case studies.

Course
Ethics in Computing
Bachelor’s students

Academic Years
2024/25

Role
Teaching Assistant

In the world of computing, ethics plays a crucial role in reflecting on how technology affects individuals and society.

The impact of computing technologies depends on how responsibly they are designed and used. This course offers a practical and thoughtful approach to understanding ethical challenges in areas like privacy, security, algorithmic bias, and intellectual property. Students explore how ethical principles influence decisions and outcomes in the tech industry.

They learn to identify ethical dilemmas, apply frameworks for responsible decision-making, and balance innovation with societal well-being. Through case studies, discussions, and real-world scenarios, students develop the skills to navigate the complex ethical landscape they will face as future computing professionals.

Course
Human-Computer Interaction Atelier
Bachelor’s students

Academic Years
2022/23, 2023/24

Role
Teaching Assistant

Essential for first-year Informatics students, HCI provides the foundation for creating smooth, user-centered digital experiences, which are critical in today’s tech-driven world.

Students delve into the field of Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), learning how people actually engage with technology and how to create software that seamlessly integrates into their daily lives. They work on real-world projects, prototyping and testing applications that prioritizes usability and user experience.

By overcoming practical challenges, students learn how to turn their ideas into unique, user-friendly solutions that engage with their audience. This course focuses on developing technology that is not just functional, but also interesting and intuitive. Students depart with the ability to design digital experiences that users enjoy.
